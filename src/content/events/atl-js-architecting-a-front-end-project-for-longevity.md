Hey Everyone! We're partnering with ATL JS, Modern Web ATL, and Vue.js Atlanta to host Sean from Chick-fil-A!

Join us at 6:30 for food and networking. The speaker starts around 7!
**Speaker:** Sean McQuaid, Lead Software Engineer, Chick-fil-A
**Architecting a Front-End Project for Longevity**
Building software is hard enough, but sculpting a sustainable, maintainable front-end project is an art form. In this talk, we'll dive into pivotal concepts for establishing a new project or revitalizing an existing one. We will discuss strategies that empower you and your team to engineer projects with the adaptability crucial for their long-term success.
**About the Speaker:**
Sean is a Lead Software Engineer at Chick-fil-A where he works on overall web architecture throughout the Operator Technology Solutions team. Prior to learning how to code, he attended music school and played the trumpet professionally for over a decade. Sean is very passionate about testing, performance and tools that provide a good developer experience.
