**Join us for an evening at the Modern Web + ATLJS + ReactJS ATL + VuJS ATL event!**
This isn't just any developer meetup—this is your opportunity to learn from and connect with some of the most brilliant minds in the JavaScript community. Featuring **five incredible speakers**, this event promises to be packed with insights, inspiration, and networking opportunities.

**Meet Our Speakers:**

1. **Homer Gaines**:

* Homer is a seasoned Front-end Developer and a Certified Accessibility Professional. He brings a wealth of knowledge in accessibility, user experience design, and front-end development. Homer is also an independent recording artist and a sought-after international speaker​.

2. **Abbey Perini**:

* Abbey is a passionate JavaScript developer known for her work in front-end development and community engagement. With extensive experience in React, Vue and modern JavaScript frameworks, she contributes significantly to the developer community through speaking engagements and workshops.

3. **Alex Riviere**:

* Alex is a dynamic full-stack developer with a knack for building scalable web applications. He has a strong background in both back-end and front-end technologies, and runs VueJS ATL. Alex frequently shares his knowledge through talks and online courses, helping developers elevate their skills.

4. **Raymond Greeley**:

* Raymond is a senior software engineer. With years of experience in JavaScript and cloud technologies, he excels in creating efficient and high-performing applications. Raymond is also known for his engaging presentations and practical coding sessions.

5. **Natalie Cole**:

* Traditional software development often treats Quality Assurance (QA) as an afterthought, leading to increased costs, delays, and compromised quality. Natalie's presentation advocates for a "Quality First" approach, embedding Quality Engineering and Assurance in every stage of the software development life cycle, therefore addressing and identifying defects early, which ensures a higher quality product and user satisfaction. She'll discuss practical strategies for transforming organizational culture to prioritize quality.
