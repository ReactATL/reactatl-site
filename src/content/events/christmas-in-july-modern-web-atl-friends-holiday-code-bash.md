Join us for a frosty twist on summer fun as **Modern Web Atlanta**, **Atlanta JavaScript**, **React ATL**, **VueJS ATL**, and **REFACTR.TECH** team up for **Christmas in July with Modern Web-ATL!** 🎄☀️
**What to Expect**

* **Cookie Exchange:** Bring a batch of your favorite cookies (homemade or store-bought—we won’t judge!) and swap with fellow devs.
* **Holiday Spirit Contest:** Rock your best “summer Santa,” beach elf or gloriously ugly holiday fit & compete for prizes (bonus points for creativity!).
* **Tech Talks:** Gain fresh insights from four local community leaders across frameworks and tools:
* **Martin Rojas** – UI Architect at PlayOn Sports & ReactATL Organizer
* **Daniel Rivadeneyra** – Professional Services Specialist at Docufree
* **Samuel Obukwelu** – Founder of Assignment Tracker
* **Elliot Fouts** – President & CTO at This Dot Labs

**When & Where**
📅 Wednesday, July 30, 2025
⏰ 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM EDT
📍 Atlanta Tech Park, 107 Technology Pkwy NW, Peachtree Corners, GA
Bring your holiday cheer (and your appetite) — we’ll handle the air conditioning!
