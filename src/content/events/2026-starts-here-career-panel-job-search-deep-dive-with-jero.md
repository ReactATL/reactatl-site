**Let's kick off 2026 the right way.** 🚀
ReactATL is teaming up with **REFACTR.TECH, ATL JavaScript, and Modern Web** for our first event of the year—and we're going all-in on the topic that's on everyone's mind: **getting hired in 2026's (let's be real, tough) job market.**
Whether you're actively searching, planning your next move, or just want to know what's actually working right now, this night is for you.

## 🎤 The Current State of the Job Search (Career Panel)

Hear real talk from people on **both sides of the hiring table**—recruiters, hiring managers, and engineers who've navigated this market successfully. We're covering:

* What's actually happening in hiring right now
* The skills and signals that get you noticed
* How to stand out when everyone's trying to stand out

**Panelists:**

* **Calypso Hernandez** – Executive Operations & Strategy Lead at This Dot Labs *(Moderator)*
* **Kelly Vaughn** – Sr. Engineering Manager at Zapier
* **Debi Creasman** – Managing Partner at Insure Technology
* **More panelists to be announced**

## 💼 A Detailed and Systematic Approach to Getting a Software Engineering Job

After the panel, **Jerome Hardaway** (Founder of Vets Who Code) is taking the stage for a deep-dive workshop on his critically acclaimed job search framework.
This isn't generic LinkedIn advice. Jerome walks through a **step-by-step, actually-actionable roadmap** covering:

* Optimizing your GitHub and LinkedIn presence
* How to highlight your experience (even if it's non-traditional)
* Networking with intention, not desperation
* The mindset and systems that actually work

If you've ever thought "I just need someone to tell me exactly what to do," this is that session.
