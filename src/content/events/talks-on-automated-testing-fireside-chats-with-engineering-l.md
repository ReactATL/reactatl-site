### 🍕 **Understanding Automated Testing: Unit, Integration, and End-to-End Testing Explained Through Pizza**

**Speaker:** Nicole Gathany, Software Engineer, MournTogether
Automated testing is key to building reliable apps, but grasping the differences between unit, integration, and end-to-end testing can be tricky. This talk breaks it all down using a simple (and delicious) analogy: **pizza.** Whether you’re new to testing or refining your skills, this session will boost your confidence in writing effective tests.
