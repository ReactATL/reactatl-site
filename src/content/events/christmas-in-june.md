It’s the most wonderful time of the year again! Join React ATL, Modern Web Atlanta, Atlanta JavaScript, and VueJS ATL as we celebrate Christmas in July with an evening of sweet treats, community connections, and festive fun!

**What to Expect:**

* Cookie Exchange: Bake (or buy, we won’t judge!) a batch of your favorite cookies to swap with fellow attendees.
* Holiday Spirit Contest: Don your best “summer Santa,” beachy elf, or ugly holiday attire for a chance to win prizes. Bonus points for creativity!
* Tech Talks: Hear from local community leaders sharing insightful talks across frameworks, tools, and trends.

Bring your best holiday cheer and we’ll bring the air conditioning!
We'll also have talks from:

**[Brandon Mathis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mathisbrandon/) \- Engineering Lead at This Dot Labs**
"Exploring Pi Coding Agent"
**Description**: I have been a Claude Code user for over a year, but recently found myself asking questions about how my coding harness worked and if I could customize it like I have my text editor. This led me on a journey to explore several open-source agents and eventually to choose Pi. I have been using Pi with GPT-5.5 for several weeks now, and in this talk, I will explain why this is my preferred coding agent, how I customize it, what customizations I have made, and how you can get started yourself.
