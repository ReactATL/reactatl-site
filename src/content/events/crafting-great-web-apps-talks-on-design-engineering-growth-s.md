### **Talk Lineup**

**From Junior to Staff - What is my actual job?**
*Speaker: Dustin Goodman, Staff Frontend Engineer at ClickUp*
Ever wondered what it takes to level up as an individual contributor? Dustin Goodman will guide you through the journey from novice to expert, sharing strategies for navigating the evolving expectations of each engineering role. Gain actionable insights for personal and career growth, whether you're just starting out or eyeing a leadership role.
**Code Meets Creativity: Design Basics for Web Apps**
*Speaker: Calypso Hernandez, Marketing and Event Coordinator at This Dot*
Design doesn’t have to be daunting! Calypso will break down design fundamentals—color, typography, spacing—and show you how to create stunning web apps that deliver an excellent user experience. From beginner tips to powerful tools (AI-powered and traditional), this talk will help you bring your web apps to life.
**Leader Fireside Chat**
*Hosted by Beth Laing, Senior Director of Engineering at CallRail*
Join Beth for an insightful conversation with tech leaders (details TBA) as they share experiences and lessons learned from leading engineering teams.
