**Join us for Engaging Talks on AI, Career Growth & More!**

We're bringing together software engineers across Atlanta for a night of insightful discussions and networking. Whether you're passionate about AI, career development, or just want to connect with like-minded developers, this is the place to be!

**Featured Talks**

AI in Education

Speaker: Evan Marie Carr, Co-Founder at DarkViolet.ai

AI is reshaping education, and developers have a unique opportunity to drive real impact. In this talk, Evan Marie Carr shares insights from Learn with Lumi, a project that blends AI and education to create personalized, adaptive learning experiences. Learn how AI-driven systems can break down barriers, improve accessibility, and redefine how we teach and learn. If you're a developer looking to apply your skills for meaningful change, this session will offer valuable takeaways.

Bridging the Gap: Thriving in Tech Careers

Speaker: Chris Nowicki, Software Engineering Lead at This Dot

Breaking into tech isn't just about coding—it's about navigating a full career transformation. While bootcamps and tutorials provide the hard skills, they often miss the bigger picture: well-being, growth strategies, and long-term success. Chris Nowicki will explore the often-overlooked aspects of career development, covering:

Identifying and addressing skill gaps

Building strong support systems

Balancing technical growth with mental well-being

Measuring progress without burnout

Whether you're a career switcher, a self-taught developer, or just looking for sustainable growth strategies, this talk is packed with insights to help you succeed.

Fireside Chat

Host: Erik Boemanns, Founder & Chief Technologist at Mirability, LLC

Stick around for an open conversation with industry leaders, where we'll dive into key trends, challenges, and opportunities in the tech world. Bring your questions and insights!
