We made it! Let's celebrate an amazing year of learning, building, and growing together as a community.
Join **ReactATL, Modern Web Atlanta, Atlanta JavaScript, VueJS ATL, Refactr.Tech, ATL .NET, Azure ATL, and ATL Hardware** for our annual holiday party—a night of festive fun, cookies, great people, and plenty of laughs.

## What to Expect

**Cookie Exchange**: Bring a batch of your favorite cookies to share with the community. Classic? Experimental? Seasonal twist? We want it all. Everyone goes home with a sweet box of treats. 🍪
**Ugly Sweater Contest**: Dust off your tackiest, funniest, or most creative holiday sweater. Prizes for the ultimate winners!
**Games, Prizes & Good Food**: We'll have snacks, drinks, games, and surprise giveaways throughout the night. Because what's a celebration without a little friendly competition?
**Real Talk**: This is the perfect time to connect with developers across our Atlanta tech communities—no lightning talks, no pressure, just genuine connection with people who get what you're building.

## Event Details

📅 **Tuesday, December 9** · 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM EST
📍 **Improving Offices** · 11675 Rainwater Dr #100, Alpharetta, GA

## What to Bring

* A batch of cookies for the exchange (seriously, bring 'em!)
* Your ugliest holiday sweater or festive fit
* Positive energy and maybe a friend or two
* Appetite for cookies (and everything else) 😄

This is one of our favorite nights of the year. Let's close out the year together. **See you there!**
