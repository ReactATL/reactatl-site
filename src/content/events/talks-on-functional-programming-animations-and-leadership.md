We're back in June for a meetup featuring two great technical talks and a chat with the CEO of Product Protege!
Join us for pizza, networking, and to learn from our local tech community. Food starts at 6:30, talk start at 7 with the following agenda:

**Leadership Fireside Chat**
**Jason Abdo,** *CEO, Product Protege*
Join us for a fireside chat and gain insights from an Atlanta engineering leader.

**"Learning Functional Programming Through Construction: First Principles"**
**Cameron Presley,** *Engineering Leader & Microsoft MVP*
This talk is a great introduction to functional programming where we build out the Mars Rover kata leveraging TypeScript and building out our understanding of immutable structures, functions, and composition"

**"Animating Anything on the DOM with GSAP"**
**Mariya Harizanova,** *Front End Developer*
This talk will cover scroll-based animations (using ScrollTrigger, of course!), cool entrance effects with timelines and staggers, and even throw in some live demos. I'll also touch on best practices for performance and accessibility.
