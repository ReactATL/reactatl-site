Get ready to jingle all the way into our annual Holiday Party & Cookie Exchange!
Modern Web Atlanta, Atlanta JavaScript, ReactATL, and VueATL are teaming up for an evening of festive cheer, tasty treats, and unforgettable fun.
**What to Expect:**

* **Cookie Exchange** 🍪: Bring a batch of your favorite cookies to share! Classic chocolate chip or wild experimental flavors—all are welcome. Let's see who brings the best holiday treats!
* **Ugly Sweater Contest** 🎉: Pull out your tackiest, funniest, or most ridiculous ugly sweater for a chance to win awesome prizes.
* **Holiday Games & Prizes** 🎁: Get ready for holiday-themed games, prizes, and maybe even a surprise guest or two.

**Event Details:**

* **🗓️ Date:** Tuesday, December 10, 2024
* **🕒 Time:** 6:30 PM - 8:30 PM
* **🏢 Location:** Improving Offices, 11675 Rainwater Drive #100, Alpharetta, GA

**What to Bring:**

* A batch of cookies for the exchange (we'll have boxes ready so everyone can take some home!)
* Your ugliest holiday sweater or cutest Christmas outfit
* A festive spirit and a sweet tooth
* A friend—the more, the merrier! Let's grow our community together.

Come celebrate with us and make this holiday season a sweet one!
